# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in contributing to AI-SRE-Ops.

## Contribution license

By intentionally submitting a contribution for inclusion in this project, you agree that the contribution is licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0, as described in the [LICENSE](LICENSE) file. No separate Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO) sign-off or Contributor License Agreement (CLA) is required.

## Before submitting a change

- Keep changes focused and follow the conventions of the files you modify.
- Add or update tests for behavior changes, and include the validation commands and results in your pull request.
- Update documentation when commands, configuration, or user-visible behavior changes.
- Do not include credentials, populated local configuration files, private/customer data, or material you do not have the right to contribute.
- Do not include generated images or other release artifacts unless the maintainers have requested and reviewed them.

## Pull requests

Describe the problem and the change, identify any compatibility or migration impact, and summarize the tests you ran. Contributions may be reviewed and revised before they are accepted.
